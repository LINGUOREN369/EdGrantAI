Title: Computing Education Research
Synopsis: The Computing Education Research (CER) program aims to develop a robust, well-prepared CISE workforce by supporting transformative projects that inform our understanding of the effective teaching and learning of computing and artificial intelligence at all levels. CER staff work throughout CISE and coordinate across the agency. 


To accomplish this mission, CER aims to: 




Promote leadership and efforts to engage communities in infusing computational thinking and artificial intelligence into education at all levels and across disciplinary boundaries. 






Develop and support sustainable communities of researchers involved in innovative and transformative approaches to computing education. 






Support projects to advance education in computing, artificial intelligence, and related emerging technologies. 






Develop and evaluate interventions to improve pathways to the future computing and AI workforce. 






Promote and enable excellence in computing education through the integration of research and education.
